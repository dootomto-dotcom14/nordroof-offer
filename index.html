WHY MOVE OFF WORDPRESS

Where WordPress is costing you.

WordPress is a solid starting point, but for a business that depends on its site working perfectly, it comes with real, well-documented costs.

Speed

Plugin-heavy WordPress builds routinely lose visitors to load times a modern stack simply doesn't have.

Security

WordPress powers a huge share of the web, which makes it the most targeted CMS for attacks and vulnerable plugins.

Maintenance

Core, theme and plugin updates need constant babysitting; miss one and something quietly breaks.

Flexibility

Every new feature runs into a plugin that doesn't quite fit, or breaks something else the moment it's installed.
